separatrix

Quantum-inspired Ising/QUBO solvers, in pure Rust.

Simulated bifurcation — the Toshiba-lineage algorithm family (Goto et al., Science Advances 2019 & 2021) — implemented faithfully and benchmarked the only honest way: next to simulated annealing, parallel tempering, and exact ground truth in the same crate.

Run it in your browser → Pick a universe and a portfolio size, then watch exact enumeration check every possible portfolio while the heuristics guess — until the universe grows and exact stops being reachable at all.

  • Deterministic per seed, no OS entropy, compiles to wasm32.
  • A quantized i128 objective designed for on-chain verification: solving is NP-hard, checking is cheap.
  • Property tests enforce that no heuristic ever reports an energy below the exact ground state.

status: v0.1 solver core, shipped. So are the other two: the walk-forward workbench (39 assets, K=8, 234 rebalances, exact ground truth on 100% of them — and exact enumeration beats every heuristic at that size), and the Solana commitment program on devnet, where the chain re-derived a committed allocation's objective and got the solver's integer exactly. The workbench study is a report, not a chain record; it is not published on-chain. Still not built, and not claimed: no browser WASM demo, no run on real quantum hardware, no solver bounty.
